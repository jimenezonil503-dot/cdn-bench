TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Know what we measure. And what we don’t.

Response time ≠ Ping

Time from starting a GET until the browser receives response headers, including browser scheduling. Not ICMP Ping, nor separate DNS, TCP or TLS timing.

Effective rate ≠ Bandwidth

Decoded body bytes divided by complete request time. Compression, caching and small files affect it. Not link capacity or billable traffic.

Same file. Different networks.

Browser cache is disabled and a random query is added. This cannot guarantee bypassing CDN caches. CORS errors and timeouts are reported, not replaced by fake data.
