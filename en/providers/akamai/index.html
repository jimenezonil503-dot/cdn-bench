Overview

Akamai serves enterprise content delivery, application performance, and media distribution workloads with detailed controls for caching, origins, traffic governance, and operations.

PositioningEnterprise global delivery

02 /

Public capabilities

  • Covers web, application, download, and media delivery
  • Rule-based configuration suits complex cache and origin policies
  • Designed for enterprise migration, governance, and operations
03 /

Best-fit workloads

Large sites, media platforms, and multi-region enterprise apps

04 /

Editorial analysis

For complex media and enterprise properties, rule governance and operations can matter more than one speed number. Test configuration releases, rollback, logging, regional origins, and contractual coverage.

05 /

Questions to confirm

  1. How are complex cache rules reviewed, deployed, and rolled back?
  2. How are media, download, and application workloads billed?
Watch-outs

Configuration and procurement can be more involved than developer-first services; include operations and contract terms in evaluation.

06 /

Measurement boundary

This profile explains product positioning and selection questions. It does not claim a global speed, uptime, security, or price ranking. To measure this provider, deploy the same public HTTPS file with CORS and test it from the locations that matter to your users.
