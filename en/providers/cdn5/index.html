Editorial analysis

CDN5 belongs on a shortlist when mitigation is a primary requirement. Validate attack handling, origin stability, and regional user experience independently of static-file delivery.

Questions to confirm

Watch-outs

Protection capacities and plan features are vendor claims; confirm mitigation design, SLA, origin routes, and compliance before launch.

06 /

Measurement boundary

This profile explains product positioning and selection questions. It does not claim a global speed, uptime, security, or price ranking. To measure this provider, deploy the same public HTTPS file with CORS and test it from the locations that matter to your users.
